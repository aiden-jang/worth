# Soft characters

The second October 10 cute pass gives Worth a more recognizable family of reactions: a slouched Meh, a pear-shaped Favorite with raised hands, and a slightly lopsided Not again. Large reactions have tiny feet and a gentle squash-and-settle motion. Compact badges use just the face and silhouette. Cream cards/dialogs have 22px corners; call choices and the landing signup are capsules.

The accepted composition, typography, palette and concise dry copy remain consistent. Larger verdict drawings intentionally add some card height. The updated taste cabinet references document these changes.
