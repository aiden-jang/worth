# Cute pass

The October 10 refinement adds softer oval faces, fuller cheeks, tiny hands on large faces, and a blush heart for Favorite. Meh keeps its flat expression and gets a small shrug; Not again gets a gentler frown. The accepted layouts, palette, typography, and copy remain consistent with the taste cabinet references.

Calls settle once when changed and tilt slightly on hover. Reduced motion disables both effects. Compact badges omit hands and hearts. A record without a call remains faceless; calls are still optional, reversible, and independent of rank.
